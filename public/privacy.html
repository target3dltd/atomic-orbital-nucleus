Nucleus privacy notice

Atomic Orbital Limited · last updated 5 November 2026

Nucleus is Atomic Orbital's internal hub for processes, tools and project sites. It is for Atomic Orbital and Target3D staff only.

What we receive when you sign in with Google

Your Google account ID, name, email address and profile picture. We use them only to recognise you, check that your account belongs to an allowed company domain, and show who is signed in. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, Calendar or any other Google data through this sign-in.

Your Pipedrive API token

The first time you sign in you connect your Pipedrive API token. It is stored encrypted against your Google account and used only to read from and write to Pipedrive on your behalf inside Nucleus tools. You can remove it at any time from Your account → Connect a different Pipedrive token.

Cookies

Nucleus sets one encrypted, HTTP-only cookie to keep you signed in (7 days by default) and a short-lived cookie during Google sign-in. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.

Where it is kept and who sees it

Nucleus runs on Netlify. Data entered in the tools (for example shared settings and dashboard entries) is stored there and visible to signed-in colleagues as each tool describes. We do not sell or share your information with anyone else.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: ashley.k@target3d.co.uk.
