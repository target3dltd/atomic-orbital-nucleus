Help ·

What this is

The operations checklists, reports and templates, rebranded to Atomic Orbital. They follow the phases of the Operations process map: enquiry, prove (pre-sale), deliver (post-sale) and support.

Using it

  • Type in the search box, or pick a phase, to narrow the list.
  • Word downloads the editable template. Preview opens a PDF to look at, or print and fill in by hand on site.
  • Download all gets every template in one zip.
  • The templates use Space Grotesk and IBM Plex Sans. If they look different on your computer, install the fonts from Marketing → Assets & Press Kit → Typography.

Changing a template

Edit the original, then run build/rebrand_checklists.py (see the Nucleus README) so the branding stays consistent, and replace the files here.
