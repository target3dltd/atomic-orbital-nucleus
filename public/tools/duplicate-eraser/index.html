Help

This desk finds organisation records in Pipedrive that look like the same client, so universities and their departments roll up into one account and repeat business reports correctly. Department detail is kept on each deal, so you can still report by department.

How to work through the list

  1. Check each group. The highlighted row is the master, the record everything ends up on. Change it with the radio button.
  2. Untick any record that does not belong. It stays as it is and the tool remembers that pair is different.
  3. Check the Department box on each row. That text is written onto the contacts (and, if you choose, the deals) that come from that record. Clear it to stamp nothing.
  4. Edit the name at the top if you want the master renamed (for example to the full university name).
  5. Then choose one of:
✓ Approve queue it for a batch merge ✗ Not duplicates hide it for good Preview merge see exactly what will change, then confirm

Missing a record? Type in the Add another account box on a group to pull any organisation into it.

What a merge does in Pipedrive

  • Moves every contact, deal (open, won and lost), lead, activity and note from each duplicate onto the master.
  • Stamps the department into the contact's Department field for every person who came from a department record, and optionally into a deal field too. If the master was itself a department, its own contacts and deals are stamped as well. Anything that already has a value is left alone, so a contact's department you entered by hand is never replaced.
  • Fills blanks on the master from the duplicates: website, LinkedIn, address, labels and custom fields such as the accounts email and phone. Anything the master already has is never overwritten.
  • Renames each emptied duplicate to zz MERGED → #master name (was: old name), so they sort to the bottom and can be bulk-deleted in Pipedrive later. Nothing is deleted by this tool.
  • Adds a note on the master listing what was merged, by whom, and how much moved.

Preview and undo

  • Preview merge reads Pipedrive and lists every change before anything is written. Nothing changes until you press Confirm merge. Merge all approved previews every approved group first and shows the totals.
  • Every merge is recorded in Merge history at the bottom with the exact records moved. Undo moves them all back, clears the department stamps and filled-in fields it added, restores the original names, and adds a note saying the merge was reversed.
  • Undo only reverses what the tool did. Deals or contacts added to the master by hand after the merge stay where they are.

Where departments are stamped

The strip under the filters sets this for everyone using the page.

  • People defaults to your existing contact field Department. The department belongs to the person, which is usually right: it's the lecturer or technician who sits in that school.
  • Deals is optional. Your deal field called Department is Target3D's own team (Tech Sales, Studio Work and so on), so the tool never offers it. If you want deal reports split by client department without going through the contact, create a Text deal field called Client Department and press Reload from Pipedrive.
  • In Pipedrive you can filter deals by the contact person's Department. A deal with no contact, or with a contact from a different school, won't carry the department unless you also stamp deals.

Only text fields can be chosen.

How groups are found

  • Same institution: names are normalised and department text is set aside ("Edinburgh University - School of Informatics" → Edinburgh), and "University of X" matches "X University".
  • Known abbreviations: UCL, KCL, LSE, QMUL, UWE, MMU, LJMU, UEA, UAL, RCA and a few others.
  • Similar spelling: near-identical names, to catch typos.
  • Shared email domain: after Scan email domains, records whose contacts use the same university domain (for example kingston.ac.uk) are grouped.

Good to know

  • Archived deals are not visible to the connector and stay on the duplicate. Check the renamed record before deleting it.
  • Email history linked only to the organisation cannot be moved by the connector.
  • If a merge is interrupted, preview it again. It re-reads what is still on the duplicate and only moves what is left.
  • Decisions, merges and undos record who made them. Everything is saved in the Nucleus, so everyone on the team sees the same progress.
