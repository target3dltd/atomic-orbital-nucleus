        Help

        The Nucleus is Atomic Orbital's home for systems, processes and tooling: our own tools, project sites and the systems we use, in one place.

        Signing in

        • Sign in with Google, using your company account (once an administrator has switched Google sign-in on; until then you sign in with your Pipedrive token).
        • The first time, you're asked for your own Pipedrive API token (Pipedrive → your picture → Personal preferences → API). It's stored encrypted against your Google account, so after that Google is all you need. Everything you do in the Nucleus happens in Pipedrive as you: deals you create are yours, notes carry your name, and you only see what Pipedrive lets you see.
        • Reset your token in Pipedrive? Click your name (top right) → Connect a different Pipedrive token.
        • Only tokens from Atomic Orbital's Pipedrive company are accepted.
        • Your token is checked with Pipedrive, then sealed (encrypted) in a secure cookie that page code can't read, and, with Google sign-in, stored encrypted in the Nucleus against your Google account. It is never saved in the page or in your browser's storage.
        • With Google, a sign-in lasts 7 days on a device. (With a token only, 12 hours, or 30 days if you tick Keep me signed in.) Sign out ends it straight away; your stored Pipedrive token stays for next time.
        • Some areas, such as the Performance Hub and other people's KPI pages, are for administrators only.

        Getting around

        • Search (the bar under the greeting, or press / or Ctrl K / ⌘K) finds any tool, project, system or Sales report by name, description or tag. Use the arrow keys and Enter, or click.
        • Your day lists your Pipedrive activities that are overdue or due today, with a tick box to mark each done in Pipedrive. Connect Google once (the button in the Calendar panel) to add today's Google Calendar events, with a Join button for Meet calls, and your Google Tasks due today or overdue, which you can tick off too. The Nucleus only reads your calendar and tasks; it never reads your email or changes events. Disconnect removes that access.
        • Jump back in shows the last few things you opened on this device.
        • Tools are grouped by area: Sales & CRM, Operations & delivery, Company, Studio & motion capture, and Marketing. Project sites and Connected systems (which open in a new tab) are listed alongside.
        • Orbit view (the spinning mark by the greeting) shows everything as an atom: tools and marketing on the inner ring, project sites in the middle, connected systems on the dotted outer ring. Hover or press Pause orbits to stop it; it stands still if your device is set to reduce motion.
        • Tools open inside the Nucleus with a bar at the top: Nucleus takes you home, the menu jumps to another tool, and Open on its own opens the tool full-screen in a new tab. The last four tools you switched between stay loaded, so going back to one is instant and keeps your place; Back and Forward work too.
        • The figures under the greeting come live from Pipedrive for you: your open deals and their value, your overdue and due-today activities, and the whole team's open pipeline.

        Theme and branding

        The sun and moon button switches between light and dark. Your choice is remembered on this device; otherwise the Nucleus follows your device setting. Every tool follows it, and switching theme inside a tool switches the Nucleus too.

        Each tool's own screens use the Atomic Orbital style and logo. Documents the tools produce for clients (quotes, proposals, SOWs and the logo on the 3D floor) keep the branding set inside each tool.

        Adding a tool (for whoever maintains the Nucleus)

        1. Put the tool's files in public/tools/<id>/ (or public/projects/<id>/) with an index.html.
        2. Add or update its entry in public/nucleus/registry.js and set status: "live".
        3. If it was built as a claude.ai artifact that uses the Pipedrive connector or a shared database, add <script src="/nucleus/claude-shim.js" data-ns="<id>"></script> before its own script. Its Pipedrive calls then run with the signed-in person's token and its data is shared through the Nucleus.
        4. If it's a Claude Dashboard, copy its page into <div id="dash-root"> of a page that loads /nucleus/dash-runtime.js and lists its sources in window.DASH_DATASETS. The Support dashboard is the example to copy.
        5. Redeploy. The full guide is in README.md.
