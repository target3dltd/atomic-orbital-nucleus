About Atomic Orbital

Short · about 50 words

Atomic Orbital delivers motion capture, 3D technologies, virtual production and volumetric capture to customers around the world. Built on the Target3D story, founded in London in 2017, its independent experts take each project from first consultation through design, installation and training, with support in the customer's time zone.

Long · about 160 words

Atomic Orbital's mission is to deliver the best innovative motion capture, 3D technologies, virtual production and volumetric capture technologies to its customers, and its vision is to be globally the most trusted supplier of those solutions.

The company carries forward the heritage of Target3D, founded in May 2017 by Allan Rankin and Ashley Keeler. Beginning as resellers of motion capture equipment, Target3D grew to operate globally from London, Valencia and Gisborne, became the leading reseller of a diverse range of 3D and immersive technologies, and the sole distributor for key brands including OptiTrack, Noitom and Captury. Its London studio is part of the Advanced Media Production network, in partnership with Digital Catapult and Innovate UK.

Customers work with a team of independent, unbiased experts with over 30 years' combined experience: consultancy, try-before-you-buy demonstrations, logistics, installation and training, wet and dry hire, and support in their own time zone.

What this is

The Atomic Orbital brand and press kit, in the Nucleus Marketing section. It replaces the 2024 Target3D kit. Everything on the page can be downloaded, and the press copy can be copied with one click.

Finding things

  • The bar at the top jumps to each section and shows where you are.
  • Download the press kit gets everything in one zip: logos, fonts, letterheads and the style guide.
  • Logos: each card shows the logo on the background it is made for. SVG for screens and print; PNG when a tool won't accept SVG.

  • Press: the tagline, boilerplates and legal footer each have a Copy button. The wording comes from the Brand Style Guide 2026. If a fact changes (offices, years, people), update the guide and this page together.
