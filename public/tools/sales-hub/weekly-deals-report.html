What counts as what — click to expand definitions
New deals — deals created (add_time) in the selected period, across Target3D UK Tech Sales - SI, PS, Studio Sales, and Iberia Tech Sales only.
Lost / Won deals — deals marked lost_time / won_time within the period, across the same four pipelines.
Support deals — counted from the Support pipeline. "New" = created in period. "Existing (open)" = created before the period, still sitting in a non-final stage.
Demos booked — projects created on the Pipedrive Projects board "Target3D UK Demo's" within the period.
Demos delivered — projects on that board that have reached the "Demo" or "Post Demo" phase within the period.
Completed projects + revenue — Pipedrive Projects marked status "completed" in the period, with a linked deal (deal_ids). Revenue is the linked deal's value. Projects with no linked deal are excluded from this count.
GP — sourced from the "Expected GP Value" custom field on the deal, same definition used in Supplier Forecast.
Currency — Iberia Tech Sales deals are priced in EUR. They're shown in their own €-labelled cards/rows and are never summed into the £ totals from SI/PS/Studio Sales.
